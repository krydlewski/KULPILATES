About the studio

Move at
your own pace.

Kulpilates is a place to make movement a habit. Join us on Tamka Street for Pilates, Gyrotonic® and yoga. Start with the basics or build on your existing practice.

Classes

Pilates

Pilates combines strengthening, mobility, precise movement and conscious breathing. Springs and a moving platform on the reformer allow us to adjust the resistance to each person.

We offer reformer, mat and equipment circuit Pilates in small groups, as well as private sessions. Beginners are welcome.

Book on Fitssey ↗
Gyrotonic®

GYROTONIC® combines flowing circular and spiral movements with conscious breathing. Specialised equipment lets you move in multiple planes to build spinal mobility, strength and coordination.

Sessions are one-to-one and adapted to your abilities and goals. No previous experience is needed.

The introductory package includes 3 private sessions for PLN 600 and is valid for one month from your first visit.

Book on Fitssey ↗
Yoga

Yoga combines movement, conscious breathing and mindfulness. In a small group, we work on strength, mobility, balance and fluid movement, with time for relaxation.

Beginners are welcome and can practise at their own pace.
