2. Before a class, tell the instructor about limitations relevant to safe exercise, to the extent necessary to adapt the activity. If there are health concerns, following a procedure or injury, or where there are contraindications to exercise, consult a doctor about taking part. Pregnancy does not automatically exclude participation; select classes appropriate to your health and inform the instructor.

3. Equipment may be used only under instructor supervision and after receiving operating instructions. Participants must not change settings, springs or equipment components without the instructor’s direction. The instructor may decline a particular exercise or suggest a safe alternative for objective reasons.

4. Clean sports clothing is required. Non-slip socks must be worn on equipment; for mat classes, follow the instructor’s requirements for the activity concerned. Outdoor shoes must be left in the designated area. After use, clean the equipment with the product provided by the Studio and return it as instructed.

5. Attendance while under the influence of alcohol or substances impairing ability, smoking, aggression, harassment, discrimination and conduct endangering others are prohibited. A person with symptoms of an infection that could put others at risk should not attend and should follow the cancellation rules.

6. The instructor may stop the participation of someone whose conduct directly threatens safety or substantially disrupts the class; where the situation permits, they will first ask the person to change their behaviour. 7. Personal belongings should be kept in the designated area. The Studio is liable for damage in accordance with applicable law, including in relation to belongings left by a Participant. 8. Hands-on exercise correction requires the Participant to be informed in advance and to consent. Consent may be withdrawn at any time. Filming or photographing other people requires their consent and must not disrupt classes.

9. Awareness of the ordinary risks of physical activity does not waive claims against the Studio or instructor for improper organisation, faulty equipment or any other culpable act or omission.
